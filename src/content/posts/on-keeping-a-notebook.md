A blank page makes no demand that the thought be finished.

That is a useful quality in a notebook. An explanation can begin with a question, a proof with a failed attempt, a project with a sketch whose labels do not quite make sense yet.

Writing creates something to return to. It lets a vague feeling of understanding become a statement precise enough to examine.

## A small practice

- Write down the question before looking for an answer.
- Keep the false starts when they reveal an assumption.
- Add an example small enough to calculate by hand.
- Leave a sentence about what remains unclear.

There is no need for every entry to arrive at a conclusion. Sometimes a good note only makes the next question easier to see.

> A notebook can be a place to think before it is a place to explain.
